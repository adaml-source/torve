Need a hand? Get support ↗
Does Torve include movies or TV channels?

No. Torve helps you discover and organize titles and connect your own sources. It does not supply media, playlists, content subscriptions, or viewing rights. Use only services and media you are authorized to access.

How will Apple Premium work?

For the upcoming Apple release, browsing the catalog will be available without a subscription. Setting up sources and playing media will require a monthly auto-renewable Premium subscription and a Torve account. Restore purchases using the Torve account associated with your subscription. Manage or cancel billing in Apple subscription settings. Third-party service charges are separate.

What happens to my account and data?

Your Torve account connects supported watch state and device settings. You can export supported account data or delete your account. Deleting a Torve account does not cancel Apple billing. See our privacy policy and account deletion page for details.
