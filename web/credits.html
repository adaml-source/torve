Inside the app previews

The phone and TV images are recreated Torve interface previews based on the Android layouts, using the licensed films above. Sample watchlists and progress illustrate the interface; they are not live catalog results or a promise of availability. Film artwork is cropped and composed with interface elements. No film ratings are invented.

Creative Commons licenses cover the credited artwork. Logos and trademarks remain with their owners and are shown only within the original posters, without an endorsement claim.
