What to expect

Why does sync require sign-in?

Sync, device linking, account-backed watch state, preferences, exports, and account deletion are tied to your Torve account so the service can enforce account ownership.

Do downloads include content?

No. Torve does not provide content, channels, playlists, streams, movies, series, IPTV subscriptions, or rights to third-party media. You are responsible for your own lawful sources.

Do donations change app access?

No. Donations are optional and never unlock features.

Free software, account-backed sync

Local app workflows do not require payment. Sign in only for sync, device linking, account-backed data, data export, and account deletion.
