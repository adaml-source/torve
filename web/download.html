Read the documentation ↗
Does Torve include movies or TV channels?

No. Torve helps you discover and organize titles and connect your own sources. It does not supply media, playlists, subscriptions, or viewing rights. Use only services and media you are authorized to access.

Do I need an account?

Sign in to use sync, device linking, account-backed watch state, preferences, exports, and account deletion. These features are tied to your Torve account.

Where can I find the source code?

Torve is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. View source and build provenance.

Do donations unlock features?

No. Donations are optional and never unlock features.
