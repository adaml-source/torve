Delete Your Torve Account

Last updated: May 2026

You can delete your Torve account at any time. The fastest path is the in-app button — this page covers what happens, what gets removed, and what to do if you can’t reach the app.

Delete from inside Torve (recommended)

  1. Open Torve on any device where you’re signed in (mobile, TV, desktop).
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The app calls our backend, which removes your account and signs every device out. You’ll see a confirmation screen when it’s done.

Delete by email (if you can’t open the app)

If you’ve uninstalled Torve or can’t sign in, email privacy@torve.app from the address you used to register. Include the line:

Subject: Account deletion request
Please delete the Torve account associated with this email address.

We confirm receipt within two business days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We’ll reply when it’s done.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted we remove, from our backend:

  • your sign-in record (email, password hash) and active sessions;
  • every device you’ve activated under the account;
  • your watch progress reports synced from any device;
  • your saved playlists and IPTV configuration;
  • any LAN-hub registrations tied to your account;
  • your account settings (preferences, profile flags);
  • queued sync events that hadn’t reached every device yet.

What we may retain (and why)

  • Purchase and entitlement records. When you buy Torve Pro through the App Store, Google Play, or Amazon Appstore, the store keeps the receipt. We retain a minimal record of the purchase + entitlement so we can honour refunds, store-mandated audits, and tax reporting. These records do not contain your name or email after deletion.
  • Billing logs. Aggregated billing logs (without personal identifiers) are kept for the period required by tax law in our jurisdiction.
  • Security logs. Authentication events used to investigate fraud and abuse are kept for up to 90 days, then purged.

Local data on your devices

Torve stores some data on the device itself — watch history kept locally, downloaded content, app preferences. Account deletion removes the server-side copy; to remove the local copy, uninstall the app. On Android: Settings → Apps → Torve → Uninstall. On iOS: long-press the icon → Remove App → Delete. On desktop: uninstall using your platform’s standard process.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store, Google Play, or Amazon subscription. Cancel the subscription in the store you bought it from before deleting your account, otherwise the store will continue to bill until the period ends.

  • App Store / iCloud: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Google Play: open Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Amazon Appstore: Your Account → Memberships & Subscriptions.

Questions
