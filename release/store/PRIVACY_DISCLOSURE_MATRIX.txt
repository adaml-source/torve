DATA TYPES COLLECTED (Google Play Data Safety):
- Crash logs (Crashlytics) — automatic, encrypted in transit
- App interactions (Analytics) — automatic, encrypted in transit
- Device identifiers (Analytics) — automatic, encrypted in transit
- Email address, optional display name, user ID, authentication state, and service emails — account functionality
- Installation/device identifiers, device type/name, app version, paired-device state, and last-seen data — device management and security
- Account-backed settings, favorites, watch state, playback progress, and sync revisions — app functionality and synchronization
- Integration metadata and credentials selected for account storage — app functionality; credentials are encrypted server-side
- User-submitted diagnostics/support reports — support and reliability
- No phone number or advertising profile is required by Torve
- NO data shared with third parties for advertising
- NO data used for cross-app tracking

The Play Console declaration must be checked against the exact release build,
Firebase configuration, backend behavior, retention, deletion flow, and Google's
current definitions before every production submission. This file is an
engineering inventory, not a substitute for completing Data Safety accurately.

APPLE APP PRIVACY (Nutrition Labels):
- Do NOT select "Data Not Collected" while Torve accounts or account-backed sync
  are available. Disclose the account identity, device, settings/watch-state,
  connected-service, support, and optional iCloud data used by the shipped build.
- Current iOS source does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics, but that
  does not make account and synchronization data "not collected."

AMAZON APPSTORE:
- No analytics or telemetry SDKs included
- Disclose Torve account identity, device registration, synchronization, optional
  credential storage, transactional email, and user-submitted diagnostics.
- Do not claim that all data stays on-device.

IN-APP PRIVACY POLICY TEXT:
- Android bundled policies now consistently disclose the Torve backend, account
  and device sync, optional encrypted credential storage, connected services,
  Google-only Firebase behavior, deletion controls, and TMDB/JustWatch attribution.
- The public website policy and every store listing must remain consistent with
  this inventory and the production backend.
