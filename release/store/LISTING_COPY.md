# Canonical Torve store listing

## Short description

Watch streams now, save favorites permanently, and enjoy live TV in one app.

## Full description

Torve brings three media workflows into one consistent experience across phone,
Google TV, Fire TV, and Windows:

1. **Watch now** — find playable sources progressively and fall back
   automatically when a source fails.
2. **Save permanently** — request a movie or season through Seerr and *Arr,
   follow its progress, then play the completed copy from Jellyfin.
3. **Live TV** — browse channels with now/next guide data, favorites, replay,
   and remote-friendly playback controls.

Your Connections center shows which workflows are ready and which setup needs
attention. Account-backed settings and connections reduce repeated setup when
you add or replace a device.

Torve is free software. There are no subscriptions, paid tiers, premium
features, or purchase requirement. Torve does not supply media or provider
accounts; users connect services and libraries they are authorized to use.
