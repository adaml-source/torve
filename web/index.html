Questions

Straight answers before you install.

Torve is free software for discovery, watchlists, and account-backed sync. It does not sell content or hide features behind payments.

Is Torve free?

Yes. Torve is free software. There are no subscriptions, paid tiers, premium features, or purchase requirements.

Why create an account?

An account is required for cross-device sync, device linking, account-backed watch state and preferences, data export, and account deletion.

Do donations unlock features?

No. Donations are optional and never unlock features.

Does Torve include movies or shows?

No. Torve does not provide content, channels, playlists, streams, movies, series, IPTV subscriptions, or rights to third-party media. Torve is a media discovery and organization tool for your own lawful sources and subscriptions.

Do I need my own services or sources?

Yes. You must bring your own lawful accounts, services, playlists, and media sources. Torve does not include any content library, TV package, IPTV subscription, stream source, or access rights.

Which devices are supported?

Torve is available on Android phones, Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV devices. Support for additional platforms may be added in the future.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app through Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page.
